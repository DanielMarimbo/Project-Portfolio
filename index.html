01 — About

Technical sales, not product pushing.

I'm a technical sales and solutions specialist working across construction, protection and finishing systems for buildings and marine-exposed assets, with a strong focus on Zanzibar's coastal and hospitality environment.

My work goes beyond selling a single product. I support clients end-to-end — starting with a site inspection and condition assessment, moving through system recommendation, and finishing with product specification and application guidance on site.

I regularly work with hotels, resorts, contractors, maintenance teams and developers on problems of durability, performance, aesthetics and long-term protection in coastal and marine conditions.

Currently delivering solutions through Nabaki Afrika Ltd, and building an independent technical consulting practice alongside it — including an early expansion into smart home and building automation for future-ready buildings.
  • 01

    Site inspection

    Walk the site, assess the existing condition and identify the real cause of the problem — not just the visible symptom.

  • 02

    Diagnosis

    Explain, in terms decision-makers can act on, what's driving the failure — water ingress, corrosion, heat load, wear.

  • 03

    System recommendation

    Match the problem to a system built for long-term performance in coastal conditions, not the cheapest quick fix.

  • 04

    Specification & application guidance

    Provide the material specification and on-site guidance so the system is installed the way it's designed to perform.
